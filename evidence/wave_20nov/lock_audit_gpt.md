## Measured sealed scores and conditional decomposition

**The sealed scores locate aggregate gains. Cohort accuracy requires a separate anchor.** Write `S_i = w N_i + (1−w) R_i` for new-cell share `w` and cohort BA `N_i, R_i`. Even with known `w`, there are six equations in twelve unknowns. A selective intervention can identify a difference under an exclusion assumption. All the differences together still leave an arbitrary absolute offset between cohorts. With only BA in [0, 100] and `w = 60/106`, cap250 permits new BA **69.94–100%** and returning BA **60.80–100%**, constrained to a single line. Adding the assumption that returning BA is at least new BA gives **69.94–82.99% new** and **82.99–100% returning**.

**Denominator check.** The owner receipts for uploads 4–6 give plain accuracy **16,386 / 20,083**, **16,459 / 20,083** and **16,662 / 20,083** to their reported precision. The transitions recover a net 73 and 203 correct windows. Cap250's third count is verified by the penultimate receipt. The counts reproduce the three published accuracy values. The denominator gives no split by cell or cohort, and plain accuracy does not enter the BA decomposition. At public visit lengths, 20,083 windows are consistent with about 106 visits. The withdrawn design implies 60 new / 46 returning visits and, assuming two interfaces per visit, uses those same cell weights. Eight one-visit public people would account for 40 of the 46 returning visits. **Reference scenario.** Set `w = 60/106`. Anchor cap250 new BA at **77.35%**, the first-visit, participant-equal full0 DEV score. It avoids giving P04 and P10 thrice the weight of other people in the 28-cell dev set. It assumes the first-visit public score transports to sealed never-enrolled people across visits. Solving the sealed equation gives **90.34% returning**. The independent conditional full0 returning P04/P10 score is **89.98%**. That approximate agreement is compatible with the scenario, with two in-lab people and eight unobserved returners. ### Podium and first-place targets

With reference cap250 at **77.35 new / 90.34 returning**, each row gives three possible pairs. The thresholds are the supplied rounded board scores. At new weights of 50–65%, bronze needs **+2.79 to +3.63 new** if returning stays flat, or **+3.63 to +5.18 returning** if new stays flat. Under the 60/46 split, earning bronze or first solely on the eight unobserved returners requires **+4.80 / +7.72 pp** on their cells. Returning alone could reach bronze in the central scenario. It could not reach first from the 95.05% returning anchor. The reference allocation places **75% of cap250's remaining error mass on new people** (63–87% across the stated order/visit anchors at fixed weight). New-person transfer is the higher-confidence target for a large general gain. The leaderboard gap itself can have either source. Open-set recognition and adaptation for the eight remain a material alternative. ## Tests on corrected shuffled delivery

Two tested ideas target the forward mixer. Four treatment arms use the same actors and run all live downstream stages, including count and CSP. They use the Lane Z corrected held-new cap250 and hardcb configuration. The historical actor tapes, incumbent shadow, gate inputs and refit targets are fixed. Test branches cannot change those trajectories. Tests cover ten reused held-new people, 28 official cells and 2,655 windows per order. Recognition and own-space are unrouted. This is a corrected post-mixer replay with real adaptation tails. Exact Solver execution, recognition changes, fresh actors and returning accuracy are outside this measurement. [Registration](../../docs/LEDGER.md#lane-lock--sparse-portfolio-and-class-specific-posterior-stack-registered-fri-20-nov-2026-before-fitting-or-scoring-either-arm) · [Lane Z pins and controls](laneZ_chain_profile.md)

- **Sparse portfolio.** Equal mean of c1, Hilbert core, EA FBCNet, Sinc and raw FBCNet; test cap250 and hardcb raw slots against their respective bases. This gives each item 20% and tests an interaction between a stronger raw group and a large simultaneous mix change. All actors still run in replay. A native runtime saving would require checking what the shadow still consumes.
- **Class-specific stacking.** Six grouped posteriors add ridge-penalized residual logits to hardcb's posterior, with a fitted interaction for expert vs mixture disagreement. An additive control uses the same features with the interaction off. Each held person's coefficients exclude that person's labels and use the opposite order. Historical base fits predicting the meta-training people may themselves include the held person. Strict nested actor refits or a fully independent cohort would be needed to qualify transfer. The live formula uses current posteriors only.
- **Smoke and recovery.** Local `replay/check.py` printed `OK`. Full0 smoke 3144928 stopped on signed values in a reconstructed c1 tap. The five-way mean went below zero on one stock and two hardcb windows. An amendment before scoring clips this derived mean at zero and renormalizes with a −0.001 guard. Within0 smoke 3144930 passed the original path. Revised jobs 3144937 and 3144938 passed their 190-row P01 smokes, then ran the full orders. They match exact stock and hardcb raw, filtered and terminal controls, verify the CBraMod delivery and Lane Z permutation, require live posteriors to change, and test prefix-invariant mixers. The three original dependent submissions were canceled. The wave used eight Slurm submissions including the canceled dependencies. **Decision on these forms.** The portfolio and disagreement stack miss the registered gain criteria on both orders. The raw upgrade transmits less in the portfolio, the opposite of the forecast. The disagreement term modestly improves the additive readout and cannot recover its loss. The sign reversal from mix to terminal shows why a raw-mix score cannot qualify a replacement. Lane Z's label-aligned perturbations usually transmitted once they reached the mix. These interventions alter the posterior distributions that feed the later stages. Calibration and pseudo-label selection are plausible explanations. The run does not isolate which stage causes the reversal. Dropping P04 or P10 separately keeps the terminal deltas negative for both portfolios and the disagreement stack.

Returning gains are unmeasured. With zero returning gain, the unverified 56.6% new weighting yields **−0.89 / −1.06 / −0.70 pp** for the stock portfolio, hardcb portfolio and hardcb disagreement stack, respectively. Legacy `J` weights person-equal new and P04/P10 returning 50/50. Its zero-returning-gain scenarios for the same three rows are **−0.85 / −1.04 / −0.66 pp**. Neither is a sealed estimate.

## Recommendation and proposed follow-up

Keep `final_stack_hardcb_safe.zip` as the candidate from this audit. Its projected 3,130–3,182 s is below the official budget and above the repository's 3,060 s margin. Its unchanged public replay and Linux GPU checks cover compatibility on those tested hosts. The official RTX PRO 4500 and private runtime remain unknown. Held-new fold evidence is modest (+0.33 pp over cap250 across four orders). The portfolio and learned stack supply no evidence for switching. [Package qualification](final_stack.md) · [Fold and decision handover](HANDOVER.md)

**Proposed, outside this wave.** Test person coverage jointly across the primary actor families and refit the source-only calibration inside each fold. First compare matched five- and nine-person rosters at a controlled training-window budget, then vary real source-person count where task and montage match. Compare the full chain with an independently trained decoder or residual branch. Score overlap on cap250's wrong windows, alongside official BA and an external cohort. The separate Team C lane measured a +8.72 pp paired label-oracle ceiling, while its causal nearest-neighbour selector lost −2.23 pp. That leaves exploitable disagreements as a research question and rules out the tested selector for the deadline. The current public roster affords no arm with more than nine same-task source people per held-new fold. A joint roster test needs new actor, shadow and calibration fits, so the eight-job wave spent its budget on two feasible mixer interventions. Continue assessing recognition and post-recognition adaptation on independently enrolled, cross-day cohorts. The one-session returners could still contribute a podium-sized change, and the current data cannot measure their later PROTEUS visits.
