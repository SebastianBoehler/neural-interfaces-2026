## #2 oracle (laneY/oracle_rigid.py)
Readout = centered log-probs projected on the 2-D simplex plane. Per cell, best rigid map (O(2) rotation/reflection + shift, small ridge on the shift) vs shift-only (what rebalance already does), with labels: in-sample (upper bound) and 2-fold cross-fit within the cell (honest label-informed ceiling). Applied to mix, tsem and terminal of stock and hardcb.
Prediction: the cross-fit rotation oracle beats shift-only by < 3 pp at terminal (the 3-D readout is not rigidly wrong after EM/TSEM); P(>= 3 pp) 15 %. Rule: continue to a label-free version only if oracle rot_cf minus base at terminal >= 3 pp (mean of both orders and both systems); report oracle minus bias_cf as well.

## #5 oracle (laneY/oracle_resrot.py)
Member: logistic regression on EA tangent features (3 bands, as laneR/oracle.py) trained on 9 people (LOPO over the ten), EA per cell with its own mean covariance. Oracle: per cell, near-identity Cayley rotation A (41x41 skew, penalty ||A||^2) of the whitened covariances, fit with labels on half of the cell and tested on the other half (cross-fit), classifier frozen. Prediction: oracle gain < 2 pp (820 parameters on ~95 windows overfit; P(>= 2 pp) 20 %). Rule: stop below 2 pp mean cross-fit gain over the no-rotation member; otherwise label-free OT version.

## #3 error-targeted member (laneY/err_member, Galvani GPU)
Member: FBLight-class / EEGNet raw member, 9-person folds over the ten held people, trained with sample weights w = 1 + k*err where err = 1 if the stock chain terminal (stored, from the OTHER folds' terminal outputs only; here the terminal output of a window of a training person comes from that person's held-out chain run, never from the held person's own run) is wrong. Prediction: standalone BA within +-1 of unweighted; "fixed-where-wrong" rate ratio vs unweighted < 1.5 (the chain's errors are person-level class-map errors); as a slot member the chain gain is within +-0.3 of hardcb (P(>= +0.3) 15 %). Gate: ratio >= 1.5 before in-chain insertion.
Keep rule everywhere (AGENTS.md): positive only if >= +0.2 on both orders with pooled paired 80 % interval above 0; within +-0.5 undecided.
