Honest selection, nested leave-one-person-out and cross-order: for held person h and test order o, choose the arm (candidates: the base itself = no correction, plus all 28 arms of the base, or all 56 for the pooled-bases row) with the highest mean gain over the base on the other 9 people on the OTHER order; evaluate it on h at order o. Both order directions are averaged. Intervals resample people (10) with replacement and the two orders with replacement, 10,000 draws, 80 % percentile; people-only interval also reported. Contrast references: `da` row vs ds_all (= da_s0disp, one fixed stand-in seed) and vs hardcb; `hc` row vs hardcb.
Reading: "widening works" needs the honest `da` selection to beat ds_all by >= +0.2 with an 80 % lower bound > 0; otherwise undecided if the interval spans +-0.5, no gain if the point is <= 0.
