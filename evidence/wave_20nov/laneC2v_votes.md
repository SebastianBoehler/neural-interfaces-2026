Instrument deviation (stated): the spec captures votes live with Lane Z's mixer hook and scores exact cap250 on NI-V B-new orders. Here the edit is an emission-only post-edit of the terminal (nothing feeds back into the chain), so it is scored offline on the stored lane P terminals of the two bases (hardcb, ds_l5, ten held-new people, full0 and within0, official cell-mean BA) with the member tapes of Lane R1 (`laneR1/data/*_w1_0_4.npz`: `member|c1`, `member|sc_fbraw` = effective raw-FBC slot of the hardcb system, pre-mixer). NI-V, cap250 stock and the live capture are NOT exercised. Votes = argmax of each member tape.
## Edit
Terminal top two a,b (terminal posterior), gap g = log(p_a/p_b), bins [0,.25), [.25,.65), [.65,inf). Bin = ordered pair x gap bin (27). Training rows weighted 1/(rows of the same class in the same person-session-interface cell), mean 1; only rows with y in {a,b} enter odds. Blind logit L0 = log((A+.5)/(B+.5)) per bin; joint logit L1 per (bin, c1 vote, fbraw vote) from counts shrunk by K=40 pseudorows split by the blind odds; d = clip(L1-L0, +-log2); logp_a += d/2, logp_b -= d/2, renormalise, argmax. Nested: held-out person j never enters its own table.
Arms per base: `zero` (must equal stored terminal bitwise), `blind` (d = clip(L0 - weighted mean gap of the bin), no votes, same dose), `joint`, `perm0..2` (vote tuples permuted within each pair/gap bin of the training rows, then fitted and applied with real votes). Sources: `other` (other order, other nine; primary: nothing fitted on the scored order), `same` (same order, other nine), `both`. `joint_sel`: grid K in {20,40,100} x clip in {log1.5,log2} x dose in {.5,1}; the cell is chosen by the mean nested-LOPO BA delta on the OTHER order only and applied to the scored order from source `other`.
