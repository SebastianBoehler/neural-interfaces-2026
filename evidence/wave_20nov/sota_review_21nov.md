### Adaptation, calibration and competition evidence

- **Neuroscience.** The Scherer/Friedrich studies report person- and session-dependent separability of cued mental tasks. PROTEUS also changes interface and feedback conditions. Use broader agreement across existing models and keep class symmetry. The recent late word-only consensus lost 0.033 joint pp on held, with 15 word repairs and 22 correct decisions spoiled. [Scherer et al., 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4436356/) · [Friedrich et al., 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3781165/) · [Final review](final_review_21nov.md)
- **Test-time adaptation.** T-TIME predicts with a source-aligned ensemble, then updates using conditional entropy and adaptive marginal regularisation. Wimpff et al. compare online alignment, adaptive batch normalisation and entropy minimisation. MOPC adds masked perturbation agreement, similarity-weighted pseudo-labels and memory, reporting 40–60 ms/trial on two MI datasets. This would add about 804–1,205 s at 20,083 windows if its per-window cost transferred. NeuroAdapt-Bench found inconsistent gains with TENT, SHOT and T3A, with gradient-free approaches more stable in its tested shifts. The present stack has prefix Euclidean alignment, SessionBN and bounded pseudo-label refits as well as SessionEM/TSEM. Current evidence favors qualifying the small returning-specific diet. [T-TIME, IEEE 2024 / arXiv Dec 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07228) · [T-TIME code](https://github.com/sylyoung/DeepTransferEEG) · [Online TTA, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18520) · [MOPC, IEEE 22 Sep / arXiv 21 Oct 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30422) · [NeuroAdapt-Bench, MLHC 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v340/lee26a.html) · [Current stack](cap250_pipeline.md)
- **Geometry and neighbours.** Euclidean and Riemannian re-centering differ in their covariance mean; both need a source/target transform available at delivery. TSMNet learns SPD domain-specific batch normalisation. OSPDIM corrects alignment under class imbalance, while the public PROTEUS interface cells are nearly class-balanced. BTTA-DG uses a Dirichlet representation and GMM decision, a close relative of the fitted output-tail idea. LAME graph smoothing, AdaNPC and TAST are interesting only with strict previously delivered neighbours in this task. Lane TC's live five-nearest agreement selector on older cap250/Team C lost −2.23 pp held-new. It differs from posterior smoothing with self-neighbours. R4's supervised same-session oracle also missed in its late-head form. [EA, He and Wu 2020](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2019.2913914) · [Riemannian alignment, Zanini et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2017.2742541) · [TSMNet, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01323) · [OSPDIM, Aug 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05315) · [BTTA-DG, ICLR 2026](https://github.com/luo-huan-123/BTTA-DG) · [LAME, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.05718) · [AdaNPC, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12566) · [TAST, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10792) · [Lane TC](laneTC_selector.md) · [R4 oracle](fable_r4_selfenrol.md)

MBZUAI, second on p-factor, released a combination of lagged-correlation features and EEGNeX with weight averaging and augmentation across its two tasks.
