## Evidence and verdicts

- **OK** means verified for the stated scope. **STALE** means the source evidence used an earlier pipeline, calibration or delivery convention. **UNVALIDATED** means a controlled test in the relevant condition is absent. **WRONG** means a stated assumption or reported calculation contradicts the code or evidence. Stock exact-worker execution establishes that a configuration ran. A matched intervention is needed to validate the value of a constant.
- Impact is in **percentage points of balanced accuracy**. I give a measured comparison when available. `Prior` indicates a planning range for a plausible change, `unknown` means the evidence cannot size it, and `0 when inactive` follows from the code. ## Repeated selection and the winner's curse

**WRONG** to treat the fixed-arm intervals as correction for the campaign's repeated selection. The public cohort is only ten people and each person's results recur across hundreds of fits, design branches, and comparisons. The 14 Nov audit already accounts for ~45 FT forms, ~10 label sources, ~15 Eye variants, ≥6 combination subsets and ~30 returning arms, before the subsequent campaign. Some are correlated or nested. Preregistration and blind banks protect each recorded trial's direction and data access. They preserve neither an untouched benchmark nor a fresh participant sample after the outcomes inform the next trial. [Historical audit](../review_14nov/dev_validity.md#3-selection-effects) · [18 Nov scope](../next_wave_16nov/scoring_audit_18nov.md#3-repeated-validation-is-being-mistaken-for-additional-independent-evidence)

- **Empirical estimate.** Historical selected-minus-nested gaps were typically **0.2–0.4 pp**. Stage retune +.44 in-sample became −.02/+.16 across people, learning-rate +.29 became ~0 in order replication, and the final four-arm selection alone cost 0.02–0.14 across the three schemes in a nested split. Those checks cover specific choices, with shared people or source fits elsewhere. They cannot estimate the entire selection effect on cap250. Cap250's +1.011 pp sealed step remains measured. [Selection audit](../review_14nov/dev_validity.md#3-selection-effects) · [Sealed receipt](../../friday/DECISION_18NOV.md#official-penultimate-receipt)
- **Sensitivity calculation.** For equally effective Gaussian candidate effects with per-comparison SE σ, a selected maximum has expected optimism `σ E[max(Z₁…Zₘ)]`. At 5, 10, 20 and 50 effectively independent comparisons, the factors are 1.16, 1.54, 1.87 and 2.25. Using σ=.20–.43 pp (NI-V seed null through fixed whole-package contrast) gives **0.23–0.80 pp** for an effective 5–20-way choice, or 0.45–0.97 at 50. For 100–300 independent choices the factors are 2.51–2.88 (0.50–1.24 pp). The latter is a pessimistic scenario for a correlated campaign. With full0 half-width implying σ≈.59, even 5–20 equal-effect comparisons can produce **0.69–1.10 pp** of apparent improvement. Neither effective trial count nor common true effects are identified. Do not subtract these figures from a sealed result or use the null MDD as a universal gate.
