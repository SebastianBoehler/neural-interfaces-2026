# Additional same-task EEG cohorts for cap250

Decision target: Friday 20 November 2026, 13:00 CET. This is a source and training-plan audit. Availability refers to a public release or an existing cluster export. An experiment result requires a separate registered run.

**Decision.** No newly verified public cohort supplies all three scalp-EEG labels. The exact-task author leads are Bordeaux BrainHero, the Graz Friedrich studies and Montréal Banville. For Friday, the two staged Shin cohorts offer the fastest distinct training contrasts. CMx7-MM is a lower-priority check because task and acoustic exposure coincide and an older teacher fit lost in the chain.

## PROTEUS release boundary

NEMAR `nm000290` is the Bordeaux competition release: 10 people, 14 sessions, 112 runs, 1.48 GB, CC BY 4.0. Its Git mirror exposes one release tag, `v1.0.0` (`9617060` peeled). It contains 41/41 target EEG sites at 500 Hz, acquired on Brain Products actiCAP slim / actiCHamp with left-mastoid reference. Both Graz and BrainHero contain visually cued non-dominant-hand kinesthetic imagery, serial subtraction, and letter-cued word generation. The model consumes a four-second crop resampled to 120 Hz. The public EEG-BIDS release omits questionnaire data. The existing 2,655-window export already covers this release. [NEMAR](https://nemar.org/dataset/nm000290) · [Release tag](https://github.com/nemarDatasets/nm000290/tree/v1.0.0) · [Challenge data table](https://neural-interfaces26.github.io/tracks.html) · [Current project state](../../docs/STATE.md)

The sealed split contains later sessions of public participants and participants absent from the public release, from the same acquisition programme. The organizers withhold the exact cohort size, identities, session allocation and delivery order. The earlier count of ten new participants was withdrawn. The sealed split is prohibited training data. A derivative mirror of `nm000290` would add zero people. [Upstream scientific FAQ at 8447e181](https://github.com/neural-interfaces26/2026-competition/blob/8447e181/codabench/pages/scientific_faq_bci.md) · [Upstream terms, Rule 4](https://github.com/neural-interfaces26/2026-competition/blob/8447e181/codabench/pages/terms.md)

Fabien Lotte coordinated ANR PROTEUS (`ANR-22-CE33-0015`) at Inria Bordeaux. The dataset credits Pauline Dreyer, Manon Bourdil, Loic Bechon, Simon Kojima, Sebastien Velut, Sebastien Rimbert, Raphaëlle Roy and Fabien Lotte. The Cortico 2025 protocol by Dreyer, Bourdil, Roy and Lotte reports a six-month campaign involving 22 participants in its HAL abstract. The planned protocol was six different-day visits, alternating mornings and afternoons, with eye baselines, two sham acquisition runs and four real-feedback runs per visit. The preliminary analysis was three six-visit completers. A 2026 Cortico analysis reports 20 completers × six sessions (120). The 2026 Graz BCI paper reports 12 completers × six (72), with the same 41 named electrodes and 500 Hz. These are reported analysis counts from a shared protocol and should never be added together or used to infer sealed counts. No exhaustive realized session total or public-to-paper roster crosswalk was located. [ANR record](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-22-CE33-0015/) · [Cortico 2025 protocol](https://hal.science/hal-05151171) · [Cortico 2026 analysis](https://hal.science/hal-05688870/document) · [Graz BCI 2026 analysis](https://hal.science/hal-05672680/document)

The November 2026 Telepresence paper is another exact-task Bordeaux lead. It reports 13 single-visit participants, 12 usable for classification, with sound and silence in BrainHero. It records 32 scalp EEG leads at 500 Hz, 24/41 shared, using left mastoid. The different cap and protocol are evidence of a separate experiment. Cohort overlap, raw licence and size are unknown. Inria's 2024 report describes adding mental calculus and word-association cues to its rhythm-game-derived BrainHero software for PROTEUS. Searches of NEMAR, OpenNeuro, Zenodo, HAL's 33 ANR-indexed records and public lab/GitHub results found no additional downloadable PROTEUS or related BrainHero recordings. The three commits after the NEMAR tag modify workflow and metadata only. **Prediction for registration.** The observed 7→9 same-task increase is +2.25 and +3.77 pp on two seeds in a single small source model. A log-people extrapolation gives roughly +1.8–3.0 pp standalone for 9→11 genuinely comparable people, then about +0.18–0.30 pp for cap250 if the prior ~0.1 standalone-to-stack attenuation holds. Effective coverage from partial, shifted tasks is unknown, so the planning predictions for full cap250 are Shin-pair +0.10 pp, Shin lexical +0.05 pp, and Ajra +0.00 pp. Use +0.15 pp for the combined Shin arm, with a plausible paired 80% range of about −0.6 to +0.8 pp. If returning gain is zero, the unverified 57/43 mix would turn +0.15 held-new into +0.09 pp overall. A paired people/order upper bound at or below zero refutes benefit in this form. A lower bound above zero plus stable direction under montage and cue controls supports it. An interval crossing zero leaves direction undecided. These are design priors, with no direct sealed or cross-domain slope measurement. [Learning curve](first_principles_fable.md) · [Current scoring caveats](../../docs/STATE.md)

**Main risk is learning the source protocol.** Changing the 22 model inputs reconstructed from Scherer moved the exact cap250-minus-v5.1b contrast by −1.128 pp [80% −2.034, −0.245] while all 19 measured sites stayed fixed. A Scherer cue-shift replication changed the external contrast by −0.883 pp after removing the direct cue overlap. Buffer against these effects by comparing the source's projection with an otherwise matched PROTEUS projection, using consistent reference handling, auditing measured-channel preservation and testing a delayed-crop sensitivity arm. Shin has 35 reconstructed inputs, making it especially vulnerable. Ajra's auditory cue and arithmetic cadence can let a model identify the task through stimulus timing. A real export smoke must prove both classes survive, the binary-loss path updates the weights, and no held public subject enters a training fold.
