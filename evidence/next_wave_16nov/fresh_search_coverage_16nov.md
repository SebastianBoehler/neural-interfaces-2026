## Contract-first framing
- Predict three cognitive commands from four seconds of 41-channel EEG, measured one second after the cue. Preserve official input `(1,41,480)` and cell-balanced accuracy.
- The independent generalization problem is transfer across people and later sessions, with two task interfaces sharing session-local state. Delivery order is fixed but nonchronological and undisclosed.
- Offline labels and permitted external data can teach representations and adaptation rules. At inference, only current/previous delivered windows of this session are available, without labels or metadata.
- Hard bounds: causal predictions; no future windows; no changing state crossing sessions, including module globals; Python entry point/one submission.py; 60-minute evaluation budget; explicit upload authorization.
- Neither linearity, covariance features, CAR, 120-Hz **training** data, fixed weights, current stages, teacher construction nor the development split is a contract requirement. Bundled native binaries are allowed, with compatibility unproven until checked.
- From these facts alone, useful alternatives include discriminative temporal/spatial learning, task/style separation, transferable external supervision, source-trained causal adaptation, and coherent session-level probabilistic inference.
- Success is a reproducible held-person/later-session full-system improvement under shuffled delivery within runtime. Numerical parity, training loss and an isolated weak-decoder improvement are separate outcomes.

## Consequential corrections and inherited exclusions
- Breadth **did** increase: §292B–D/293 evaluate coherent alternative decoders, changing representations or objectives. Code and complete matrices support this, unlike merely opening new lanes.
- Breadth remains concentrated around covariance geometry, net posterior mixing and entropy/pseudo-label updates. §301/309/311 are largely numerical/systems work; §308 changes heads on the same 44,239-vector.
- The 13-Nov map's “unlabelled data none”, “stacking none” and “whole alternatives only add-ons” are stale. Later SSL, stacks and independent decoders exist; historical labels must not be copied forward.
- “No useful independent member” is too strong: REVE pretraining is useful relative to scratch, while full-system usefulness remains unproved/negative in tested integrations. Independence without accuracy is insufficient.
- §280's claim that REVE cannot fit runtime slack does not follow from a forward-cost ratio alone: its own recorded 1.446ms/window needs end-to-end accounting with actual windows, seeds and refits. No deployment feasibility claim follows either way.
- Data-path uncertainty matters: §282 calls development windows spline-interpolated; §306 says ordinary PROTEUS export is not shown to interpolate and separates external mapping. Do not treat interpolation as an established explanation for model failure; trace owning export before a representation diagnosis.
- Mean subtraction/CAR/per-window z-score are scientific choices. §280 F5 loses about seven points versus causal EA and §282 mean-only decodes 46.06%; invariance can erase signal. These facts do not establish a confound or causal biomarker.
- Native fixed-head feature parity does not validate native training parity. Original training stopping mismatch failed; §305 tests a converged alternative. Native CPU inference is faster in one paired scope while full-solve training is slower (OVERNIGHT evidence).
- Strong-core “new method” comparisons that keep old PC128/shadow/gate are posterior interventions. They cannot refute a representation whose hypothesis requires those downstream stages to change.
- Two returning people, dependent seed bases and repeated outer-person selection limit confidence. Report official equal-cell effects beside people/order intervals and J; 57/43 cohort weighting is an inferred sensitivity analysis, not disclosed allocation.
