All four marginal point predictions held. BrainHero calibration leaves the joint positivity claim undecided. The unrestricted minimum was −0.141 pp. Selecting the weakest stratum separately in each nine-person source sample and evaluating that choice on the omitted person gave +1.370 pp, equal-weighted over ten people. The unfavorable stratum selected on the whole cohort therefore warrants caution. The [per-person choices](mix433_result.json) retain the individual results.

**Interpretation.** With the observed conditional accuracies fixed, 65.37% of total probability would need to move to reach zero overall gain. Total variation counts each transfer once. This is a sensitivity radius for weights, not an estimate of an anticipated deployment shift. The metric officially weights interfaces equally when both are present. This stress changes how often interface/mode/scheme combinations contribute to evaluation. Changing the EEG presented to a stateful decoder could also change predictions and requires a new replay.

**Coverage.** Both interfaces and both acquisition modes occurred in all 14 sessions, with all three classes in every stratum. The released split labels place P04/P09 in test, P05/P08 in validation and the remaining six in training. These historical leave-one-person-out predictions cover all ten, who have informed development. There is no public third interface or independently held interface in this comparison. Session lengths, sensor hardware, unseen interfaces and the sealed delivery distribution remain untested here.

**Reproduction.** Registration e26ef86. Run a fresh project replay into `runs/mix433/replay.txt`, then run [score.py](../../studies/18nov/mix433/score.py) using the §419/§430 inputs. The scorer verified pinned orders, chain controls, ZIP and §430 output hashes, row-to-person/class/cell metadata, and both absolute §430 endpoints to 1e−10 percentage points. It distributes each official class/cell weight across its actual calibration and online windows. Each of 6,000 paired draws resamples people and orders within scheme, then recomputes the least favorable mixture. These descriptive intervals reuse selected development data. Independent linear programs reproduced the 20% minimum and the distance to zero to 1e−10. No training, GPU allocation or upload.
