## Which inputs changed

The original §281 per-order input manifests resolve a discrepancy in §419. The historical candidate averaged eye seeds 0–8 in every order. It averaged rest seeds 0–8 in within0/within1, and 0–2 in the other six orders. Within0/within1 carry one sixth of the overall scheme-equal measure together. The §237 returning logs record the same pattern. The staged cap250 ZIP contains eye_s0–2.pt and rest_s0–2.pt. This test changes the corresponding historical inputs to 0–2 on all orders.

The [eye producer](../../studies/13nov/ocushuf.py) computes causal session trace statistics in delivery order. Its [training loop](../../studies/12nov/ocunet.py) fits on the other nine people's rows and restores held-person posteriors to recording index. The [rest producer](../../studies/13nov/restshuf.py) streams held-out sessions in their pinned order. Its [four-class training loop](../../studies/11nov/rest4/rest4.py) adds only pre-cue Graz windows associated with the training rows and emits three-class probabilities. The reconstructed masks exclude the held person's task and rest rows in every fold. The original saved streams carry window indices and probabilities but no embedded fit-row or delivery-order hash. Their identities come from the authenticated historical chain manifests and each source producer's code. The archive's actual auxiliary nets were separately fitted on all public people.

## Checks and scope

The [runner](../../studies/18nov/aux430/run.py) uses the original B2_pool chain function, all six main seeds and old-arm gate. It reruns the count filter and package-verified causal personalisation for each candidate. The nine-eye historical P0r and V6Fr arrays and final candidate scores must reproduce bitwise, including the independent §318 zero control. The old arm, gate inputs and gate decisions must remain equal after replacing the auxiliary means. All 14 sessions on each of the eight orders must have a zero-routing receipt. Fresh recording-order replay printed OK. These checks establish a paired historical new-person comparison. Transformer and shallow EA slots still retain their recorded-order behavior. Public folds informed past model selection. This study does not measure returning-person gains, sealed performance or exact public ZIP accuracy.
