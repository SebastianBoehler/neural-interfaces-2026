## Decision

Keep the registered §379 learned versus explicit versus static session-state comparison. The clearest distinct literature-derived follow-up is to test **within-window temporal scale** while holding session policy fixed. Compare jointly trained raw and aligned short/long spectral branches with a width/parameter-matched two-raw-branch control on the same held-nine folds, seeds, optimizer steps and orders. A matched time-bin permutation of the short spectral branch distinguishes useful temporal alignment from extra frequency summaries. Predict +0.3 pp over both controls on held-new-person official cell BA. A nonpositive upper paired interval against the two-raw control sets aside this design. A zero-spanning estimate is undecided. For a replacement decision, compare each complete decoder with cap250 and JS on the same held rows. This proposal became the separately registered §380 whole-decoder trial with pinned source episodes and eight delivery orders. [§380 implementation and results](scale380.md)
