## Primary sources checked and transfer limits

[Alignment De-Identifies Without Aligning,11Nov2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.15204v1): fixed invertible
per-person covariance congruences preserve AIRM condition-distance matrices. Its CKA regularizer can narrow
a reported gap by reducing reliability rather than improving agreement. The result does not prove all
learned alignment ineffective: vectorized rotations and general nonlinear maps are outside that theorem.
We had already read this paper in earlier blocks; the present revisit connects it to349/352, not a newly
discovered publication. A lower identity probe or lower alignment loss alone cannot be our success measure.

[DEFT,10Oct2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14497v1) uses a recursive Gaussian content/style factorizer,
window-level temporal-variance constraints, subject contrast and reconstruction. AppendixF includes sampler,
parameter-matched MLP, same-loss factorizer and additional-backbone controls. Variance-only hurts its mental-
arithmetic setting while the full objective helps; it is not a universal normalization prescription.
Our292C used two small residual branches, contrast/reconstruction and a weak46–48%CBraMod decoder, without
the paper's Gaussian recursion/temporal mechanism. Thus292C refutes that small implementation, not full DEFT.
A faithful retry still needs a stronger matched vanilla backbone and an equally sized/sampled control.

[Backbone-Informed Adaptation,4Nov2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07192): the abstract reports no consistent
advantage over parameter-matched tokenwise MLP adapters across two backbones/three task families. Only the
abstract was used here; no claim to reproduce its full protocol. This is a control requirement, not a reason
to reject every structured adapter. Its use of “native” describes backbone-informed architecture, not C++.

[LEAFv3,31Oct2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.24302v3) and [author project](https://leaf-bci.github.io/index.html):
fixed task instructions/text prototypes supply semantic supervision, differing from generic waveform pretexts.
Main supervised results train EEGencoder+MLP; that path removes theQ-Former, so those gains are not evidence
that inference-time prompting itself helps. Its held-dataset direct-inference appendix is a distinct experiment.
The200Hz/65channel interpolation and16dataset label semantics differ fromPROTEUS. No checkpoint license,
training-overlap audit or local compatibility was established here; no download/import was launched.

[MRieHy,17Aug2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16134v1) combines covariance and deep-feature hypergraphs,
using a FIFO target buffer. Its stated source setup is multiple labeled days of the SAME person; this is
not evidence of new-person transfer. A shuffled-prefix support graph could fit our contract, but chronological
buffer assumptions and any updating of already-emitted labels must be removed/tested. No direct reproduction
or claimed published gain for our challenge follows.

[Dual graph/convolution decoder,12Nov2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.16609v1) uses region pooling and graph
relations beside a convolution branch. Its experiment uses physical execution, not imagery, with cue−2→+2s
windows and movement-versus-rest. It supplies an architectural lead, not a comparable benchmark. Our montage,
WORD/SUB tasks and cue+1→+5s require their own intact-EEG control; graph edges are not neural causality.

[REVE attention-map probe,6Nov2026](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.05.764629v1): primary
abstract describes first-layer attention-derived channel matrices and a symmetry head criterion. Full HTML/PDF
were inaccessible. This is an abstract-only lead; computational and accuracy headlines are not adopted as facts
about our model. A different readout remains plausible despite pooled-embedding failures, pending full methods.

[ASPEN,18Feb2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16147v1) combines STFT and raw streams multiplicatively.
This predates the current week and overlaps spectral/temporal routes we already tested. Paper prose calling
multiplication an artifact-suppression AND gate is interpretation, not established robustness for our artifacts.
[Bispectral reach-to-grasp study,3Feb2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04018v1) studies higher-order coupling
in a different movement setting. Our26/313 already examined coupling/lag features; another renamed spectral
feature bank would not establish breadth. It needs a new estimator/control, not novelty by terminology.
