## Ranking the bottlenecks

**1. Domain and task coverage.** Which differences between recording studies and between people preserve the target label? The external experiment gives us a concrete warning. Features trained from separate arithmetic/rest and word/baseline contrasts ranked WORD/SUB correctly on Scherer (59.16% within-run AUC) but reached 50.78% on PROTEUS. Four native electrodes were shared across all corpora. Those results cannot distinguish missing spatial information, participant shift, or a difference between cognitive tasks. Next compare four versus broader native montages on identical people and task contrasts, with equal model capacity and a random-four control. Rotating which people we hold out of PROTEUS does not create an independent study. [§393](semantics393.md)

**2. Learning an invariant and keeping useful information.** Tonight, preserving A6's original source predictions while fine-tuning improved its learned-normalization model over an equally strong parameter penalty on both seeds (+1.145 and +0.484 individual-model points). The explicit-normalization controls did not reproduce that comparison. This gives task-preserving supervision a stronger case than blind removal of subject information. It still has to produce corrections in the full decoder. DEFT is an October preprint accepted at NeurIPS 2026. It separates task-related and subject-related features using dedicated branches; a fair implementation here needs a matched-capacity plain adapter and evidence on held people that task information survives. NeuroAtlas, also older than the search window, found that model rankings depend on dataset and that generic time-series models can be competitive. [A6 results](anchor394_result.json) · [DEFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14497) · [NeuroAtlas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14698)

**3. Generating variability at the right level.** The November 11 WGAN-GP paper reports improved within-person motor-imagery accuracy over its real-only CNN. The November 16 class-mixup paper uses two modalities and a binary task. Neither establishes benefit for a new person receiving shuffled three-class EEG. Prior §279/287 Fourier mixing and §153 MixStyle vary a window's spectrum or features. A genuine session can share a nuisance shift across many windows. We therefore ran a different experiment on all ten held people using measured source-session spectral shifts. At identical marginal shift magnitudes, a coherent shift beat shuffled per-window shifts by +0.708 pp [80% +0.162,+1.432] as a whole decoder. Versus repeated real examples it gained only +0.063 [−0.137,+0.247]. Its 5% addition to frozen cap250 was +0.029 [−0.149,+0.225]. This tests a low-dimensional spectral model; it supplies no estimate for a waveform GAN. A more ambitious generator should beat a real-data-only model with the same training updates, held-out people and held-out study, and improve at least one real deployed branch. A published motor-imagery forward simulator offers a way to vary sensor mixing separately from task dynamics; mapping mental subtraction or word generation to artificial sources would first require validation on real tasks. [§396 results](synthetic396_result.json) · [WGAN-GP](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-78150-9) · [Cross-subject class mixup](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20853) · [Earlier simulation framework](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2022.951591)

## Which early conclusions should change?

- **Reopen the synthetic-session question at the episode level.** §396 discriminated coherent from independent perturbations while failing to beat duplicated real data convincingly. Test new synthetic mechanisms against that strong no-augmentation control before investing in a generator.
- **Reopen cross-study cognitive supervision with an electrode-coverage control.** §148 originally encountered unavailable data; §352 demonstrated external binary discrimination, §356 lost points in the opposite terminal-fusion direction, and §393 now exposes a measurable cross-study discrepancy. A fixed four-electrode assay alone cannot identify the cause. [External result](external_binary356.md) · [New two-instrument test](semantics393.md)
- **Keep the exact negative result for generic REVE distillation.** §280 measured a real pretraining advantage over random initialization. §283 then tried distillation. In the six-seed complete system §292A's distilled EEGNet lost 0.140 pp against its matched plain student [80% −0.202,−0.075]. The new public corpus enables provenance-controlled and task-specific training. It does not overturn that deployed comparison. [Experiment ledger, §280/283/292A](../../docs/LEDGER.md)
- **Reopen session supervision at the level of actual decisions.** A November 15 dynamics paper improved held likelihood without a measurable decoding gain. Tonight our ordinal-transition model beat scrambling by 3.15 pp yet scored 37.18% versus 76.28% for the frozen system. Fit a history model on a genuinely separate source person's later decisions, then test its own downstream personalization path; §395/395c now implement that distinction. [Recent paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20164) · [Whole-model motif test](motif391.md)

The repeated public cohort, small genuine returning-person sample and unknown private runtime remain separate limits. We can reduce uncertainty by testing on genuinely different people or studies and by replaying both packages. Only the authorized penultimate submission can measure the aggregate change on the sealed population.
