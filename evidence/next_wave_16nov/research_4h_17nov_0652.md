## Two numerical counterexamples checked during this block

A deterministic2×2 SPD calculation used P=diag(1,4), Q=diag(4,1), W=diag(2,.5).
AIRM(P,Q)=**1.9605162869370942**, identical after the common congruence W.
Apply the actual k2 log-Euclidean prefix form instead: R1=diag(1,4^(1/3)), R2=diag(4^(1/4),4^(1/4)).
The distance between individually whitened P and Q becomes **1.6419120697416296**.
This is an algebra counterexample to extending a fixed-map theorem to changing prefix maps, not an EEG gain.
Enumerating all6rank orders of3edges gives Spearman support **{−1,−.5,.5,1}** without ties.
Therefore a3class rank-RDM metric should not serve as the main sensitive test of geometry improvement.
Use cross-validated distances/directions with reliability beside task decoding; do not optimize a noisy RDM score alone.
