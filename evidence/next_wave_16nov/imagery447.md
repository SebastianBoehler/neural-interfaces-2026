## Protocol and limits

PROTEUS instructs participants to imagine movement of their non-dominant hand kinesthetically. In Chang et al., all participants were right-handed. The same left square instructed a left-hand grasp and release in the two paradigms. Participants imagined it in one and performed it in the other. The study specifies imagery without overt movement but does not document instruction in a particular imagery perspective. Its Figure 1 places execution before imagery. Session context, practice, fatigue, attention to the cue, and physical movement can contribute to differences. Ordinary rest lasted two seconds. It cannot supply a contiguous four-second comparison window. [PROTEUS instructions](https://github.com/nemarDatasets/nm000290#readme) · [Chang et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06147-6) · [Raw data, CC BY 4.0](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28831730.v2)

This establishes separation from the identified Chang export and packages. It cannot check overlap with unidentified people in other datasets.
