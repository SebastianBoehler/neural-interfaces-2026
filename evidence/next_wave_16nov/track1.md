## Arms and interpretation

All comparisons use six distinct seeds and eight pinned orders. Control is the pinned v6.6 cap-250 system. Add arms insert one base-only family at the mean weight of the four existing SessionBN slots; swap arms replace the FB slot at its exact weight.

| Arm | Change |
|---|---|
| E | Add plain EEGNet. |
| D | Add the matched EEGNet student distilled from a leave-person-out REVE teacher, T=2 and KL weight 0.5. |
| C3A / C10A | Add FBCNet with CORAL strength 3 / 10. |
| C3S / C10S | Replace FB with its same-seed CORAL3 / CORAL10 variant. |

The D-minus-E contrast holds family, seed, fold and outer slot weight fixed; teacher loss is the intended changed component. The CORAL swaps also remove the existing FB fine-tuning contribution, so they are joint package changes, not isolated estimates of CORAL training. The add arms retain the shipped FB fine-tuning. The grid's draw-1/2 screening streams do not match the draw-0 chain inputs. Seed-3–5 distillation requires 30 additional REVE teacher fits; the previous teacher files cover seeds 0–2. Teachers' training targets exclude the held-out person, though the old script computes a separate held-out sanity prediction which must never be used as a target or for selection. Teacher targets for training people are in-sample to that teacher, not cross-fitted.

Proposed predictions in balanced-accuracy points, with 80% reading ranges: E +0.10 [−0.5,+0.7]; D +0.20 [−0.5,+0.9]; D−E +0.10 [−0.4,+0.6]; C3A +0.10 [−0.5,+0.7]; C3S 0.00 [−0.5,+0.5]; C10A +0.15 [−0.5,+0.8]; C10S +0.05 [−0.5,+0.6]. These are predictions, not observed effects. A missing or failed provenance/causality check invalidates an arm; an interval crossing zero remains undecided.

Under each outer-slot change, retain the shipped v5.1b shadow gate, r15c250 constituent streams, pinned permutations and session reset. Recompute downstream chain and causal personalisation from that arm's own delivered posteriors; do not reuse control pseudo-labels or CP outputs. This new-person all-LOSO pipeline emulates the dev route; it does not establish package-gate or returning-tail parity. Full returning validation still needs fold-specific models, base-specific prefixes, package-faithful fine-tuning for all required architectures, recognition boundaries and routed tails. The diagnostic Sinc reconstruction failed parity and must not be substituted.
