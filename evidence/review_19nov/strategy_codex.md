## 1. Diagnosis

1. **Most late work changes too little of the accuracy problem.** The team has tested many mechanisms. Too many shared a weak starting decoder, a small fixed terminal blend, or the same adapted teachers and public people. F1–F4 scored 38.82–43.92% alone. F9 reached 48.59%. A6 joint fit reached 59.7% versus 60.8% for explicit A6 and 81.2% for the complete chain on the same four people. A fixed 0.20 terminal correction answers one integration question. The newer actual five-slot replacement is stronger evidence. It got about +0.3–0.4 pp on corrected held-new replays and +0.050 pp [80% −0.370,+0.456] in the independent Scherer ZIP run. Replacing five peers with complementary models and fitting for their effect on the real mixture remains a bigger change. `friday/RETROSPECTIVE_18NOV.md:7–16,25–31`, `evidence/next_wave_16nov/joint382.md:5–26`, `evidence/next_wave_16nov/scoring_audit_18nov.md:77–90`, `friday/OVERNIGHT_TOP3.md:41,125–134`.
2. **Dev has directional value and weak calibration.** The roughly 76% historical dev headline is mostly held-new PROTEUS. Sealed 82.99% averages a different population and interfaces, including returning people. The difference between levels cannot tell us how changes transfer. The six completed uploads rose monotonically on the post-rescore backend, and all five recorded dev deltas pointed in the eventual sealed direction. However, this is a selected lineage of dependent comparisons. Dev J, the historical weighted mix, and the corrected matched instruments differ. Across the five transitions, the sealed/dev ratio ranged from 0.41 to 0.94. Cap250 was 0.91. The latest public estimate was close, while Scherer understated this specific sealed gain. Neither instrument calibrates the next delta or variation across unseen people. `docs/SEALED_UPLOADS.md:190–204,291–308,419–435`, `friday/DECISION_18NOV.md:30–36,90–92`.
3. **The measurement loop reused people and understated delivery differences.** Two historical aligned neural branches ran in recording order before their outputs were shuffled. In one real replay, the first delivered row inherited statistics from 94 unavailable windows. Corrected cap250–v5.1b full0 was +0.479 pp [−0.222,+1.452] with shared fresh Sinc. The later common-source cap250–P0r pair gave +0.667 full0 and +2.546 within0. Treat those as order-specific matched instruments, with corresponding fresh anchors. Repeated ten-person PROTEUS, two-person returning and nine-person Scherer comparisons expose fragility. New seeds and deliveries cannot establish new-person transfer. `evidence/next_wave_16nov/scoring_audit_18nov.md:7–38,61–75`, `friday/OVERNIGHT_TOP3.md:15–22`, `docs/STATE.md:5–11`.
4. **Systems effort kept packages viable, but recent parity work has a low score return.** The earlier fast solver cut a 3,732 s rescored workload to 1,129 s in a comparison that also changed routing. This bought real deployment margin. The latest matched C++ port gives identical labels and work counts, plus 1.05% ordinary and 3.23% forced-adaptation A100 solver savings, one pair each. Those are zero measured accuracy gain and roughly 27–83 s if the same ratios applied to cap250's sealed duration. The score pays for correct labels. Preserve entry-point, reset, activity, and deadline checks. Put further port and tolerance investigations on the release path only when they resolve a meaningful label, state, or runtime issue. No campaign-wide GPU-hour or token inventory supports a precise spend percentage. `docs/SEALED_UPLOADS.md:293–309`, `evidence/next_wave_16nov/native366_worker.md:9–38,48–66`, `friday/RETROSPECTIVE_18NOV.md:18–31`.
