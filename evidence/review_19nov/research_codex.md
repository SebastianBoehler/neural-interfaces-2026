At the approximate 57/43 new/returning mix, that requires **+3.01 pp on new people** if the returning effect is zero. ## Data that can add independent people

**Readout.** Overlap is the number of exact EEG electrode names in common with the local 41-channel export after case normalization. Session counts describe the published design. Secondary transfer tests use corpus-specific labels, held participants, and a frozen comparison. No speech or rest surrogate is assigned a PROTEUS label by dataset identity. Effect estimates are full-system held-new BA unless a diagnostic endpoint is named.

## Released or technically documented encoders

**How to read the numbers.** Scores in each row share that paper's task and reported protocol. Baseline provenance is stated when recovered. Bold numbers are the proposed model's author-reported scores. Blank comparisons are genuinely unavailable. Frozen linear probe means the encoder weights are fixed. Fine-tuning changes encoder weights using source labels unless the row specifies same-person labels. A leave-one-person-out (LOPO) score can still overlap the pretraining corpus. All proposed pp are incremental to the full stack on held-new public people. Inference cost is unmeasured on our RTX unless stated. FP32 weight sizes are storage estimates, separate from forward latency and memory.
