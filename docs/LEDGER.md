## F1 / F2 · Whole waveform decoder with session prototypes

Registered 18 November 2026 before any held score. In both arms, train an entire fast decoder from the official EEG tensor. The encoder learns spatial projections of the complex Fourier coefficients, then reads eight bands of log power and relative power. Mixing sensors before computing power preserves signed cross-channel relationships. A static head supplies soft pseudo-classes. At inference only already delivered EEG updates class-specific embedding sums, pseudo-counts and a shared session context. Shrink all context toward zero at the start of a session. Unlike the spectral kernel in §454, the decoder stores a fixed-size summary and learns its signal representation and readout jointly. The waveform studies §439/441 tested gain augmentation with a different memory, and §449 centered A6 writes after fitting. That change tests whether earlier trials indicate session style after removing predicted task composition. The prototype writes remain identical in form. ## F3 / F4 · Learn where within the EEG window the signal changes

Registered 18 November 2026 before opening scores for these actors. The F1 waveform encoder discards changes across the four seconds before reading band power. Within-window progression may separate lexical retrieval from sustained imagery or arithmetic. §389b split class-conditioned autoregression into halves and its timing effect varied by seed. §380 used multiscale carriers and a short-branch permutation. F3 retains waveform-trained spatial projections and prototypes and learns on two independent contrasts of four one-second power frames. Both arms see the identical whole-window spectrum and amplitudes. `chronological` receives signed late-minus-early and interior-minus-edge power contrasts. `symmetric` receives absolute late-minus-early and signed interior-minus-edge contrasts. The latter is invariant under frame reversal, including within the first/last pair. Both have the same capacity, initialization, source rows, shuffle and training budget. FFT magnitudes alone cannot resolve the direction of the discarded contrast. The signal model may learn artifacts in fixed post-cue timing, so a positive result advances only with the perturbation control below and external verification.

- F3 predicts chronological minus symmetric +0.75 pp (80% −0.75 to +2.25) whole genuine, +0.12 (−0.40 to +0.64) in full cap250 insertion, and +0.10 (−0.40 to +0.60) whole history-specific difference in differences. Keep F1's fit/exclusion/eight canonical deliveries/common metric/terminal weight/control seeds and check independent direct frame reversal. Both seed lower 80% endpoints above zero support a directional contrast, both uppers at or below zero refute it, otherwise undecided.
- F4 trains the same chronological model after shifting within-window source frames by −30/−15/0/15/30 samples (−250 to +250 ms at 120 Hz) with reflection at the true window edges. Each block of five episodes gives every within-episode position each shift once. `coherent_time` shares an offset across the episode and `scattered_time` rotates the same block's offsets across windows. Episode rows, seeds and steps are identical to F3. Evaluate original, unshifted probe tensors only. Predict coherent minus scattered +0.45 pp (−0.8 to +1.7) whole genuine, +0.10 (−0.45 to +0.65) full genuine, and coherent minus unaugmented chronological +0.10 (−0.45 to +0.65) full genuine. Also report both crossed genuine-minus-blank changes to locate memory dependence. Same uncertainty rule, no adaptation to held labels or score-selected results.
- Four 20-fit arrays capped at four concurrent each, following one 4-step real H100 smoke per arm. Fit wave F4 after F3 begins. Pair candidate controls in independent Galvani held-person full-stack replays and record whole decoder evidence separately. Source code, data, epoch steps, fit exclusion, independent prefix/reset/single-window checks and blind score bank are mandatory. Reserve paired A100 timing before any release. No upload.

## F5 · Train a waveform correction on fit-excluded cap250 mistakes

Registered 18 November 2026 before training and external scoring. F1's waveform member reduced full public cap250 performance. F5 trains the chronological waveform encoder and causal prototypes to correct the historical three-eye/three-rest cap250 posterior directly. §376 used a spectral proxy and frozen representation. F5 uses actual historical fit-excluded predictions and trains the entire waveform decoder. All ten public people are training data. The holdout is independent Scherer EEG in the exact staged cap250 worker. §430 route and component-order caveats apply to the training supervision.

- Match seeds 460/1460, initialization, class/interface-balanced loss, 600 complete source sessions sampled from the eight pinned orders, AdamW 0.001/0.001. Map cap250 outputs by row and stream age. Train with cross-entropy after adding 0.20 times centered raw waveform logits clipped to ±3. The `true` arm uses actual cap250 log posteriors and `wrongswap` swaps the two incorrect class logits per source window. The swap preserves true-class probability, confidence distribution, entropy and the incumbent error indicator. `ordinary` uses waveform CE alone. All arms have the same static and template auxiliary losses. Training uses no Scherer waveform or labels.
- Compare full terminal insertions with genuine or blank history. Score two canonical Scherer orders and both days of all nine people against staged stock. Predict true−wrongswap +0.20 pp (80% −0.60 to +1.00), true−ordinary +0.55 (−0.30 to +1.40), true−stock +0.18 (−0.60 to +0.96). Positive lower paired people/order bounds on both seeds support a contrast, nonpositive uppers on both refute it, otherwise undecided. Genuine minus blank is secondary. Source losses measure optimization.
- Galvani CPU extraction authenticates §430, pinned ages, unchanged decisions and fresh replay OK. Ferranti four-step real smoke per objective, then six five-minute H100 jobs. Record source/weight hashes, parameter updates, corruption parity and one-window equivalence. Real external smoke in clean A100 worker, nine paired workers and collector. Require stock/zero identity, order/counter match and blind decision proof. No upload.

## F6 · Supervised motor imagery pretraining across corpora

Registered 18 November 2026 after F1–F4, before F6 fitting. The preceding compact decoders used true history to improve on some blank controls, but all full-stack insertions trailed cap250. F6 tests whether supervised sensorimotor pretraining supplies a missing representation. Pretrain the chronological encoder and spatial projections on five motor/rest targets from PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery v1.0.0, subjects 1–30, three existing exports, 5,220 windows with no channel interpolation. Restrict pretraining to spectra from 7 to below 30 Hz to favor sensorimotor rhythms. Use unchanged PROTEUS EEG at fine-tune and replay. §277 used self-supervised reconstruction and contrastive learning.

- Pretrain `motor_transfer` with authentic labels and `motor_null` with within-person/run target permutations. Both arms use identical inputs, label counts, initialization, and 1,200 updates (AdamW 0.001, decay 0.001, batch 96). Discard the external head. Refit the entire chronological decoder with the F3 source-held PROTEUS recipe, 600 updates and seeds 460/1460 on Ferranti. Replay ten held folds and eight pinned orders, whole model and full three-seed cap250. Compare also with the authenticated F3 no-pretrain chronological result.
- Predict authentic minus shuffled +1.5 pp (80% −0.5 to +3.5) whole, +0.15 (−0.35 to +0.65) full, +2.0 (−1.0 to +5.0) whole motor imagery recall and +0.30 (−0.4 to +1.0) full motor recall. Both positive lower paired people/order bounds support, both nonpositive uppers refute, otherwise undecided. A full insertion that trails stock does not advance. Source pretraining loss measures optimization only.
