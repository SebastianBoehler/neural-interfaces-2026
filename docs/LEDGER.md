## F1 / F2 · Whole waveform decoder with session prototypes

Registered 18 November 2026 before any held score. In both arms, train an entire fast decoder from the official EEG tensor. The encoder learns spatial projections of the complex Fourier coefficients, then reads eight bands of log power and relative power. Mixing sensors before computing power preserves signed cross-channel relationships. A static head supplies soft pseudo-classes. At inference only already delivered EEG updates class-specific embedding sums, pseudo-counts and a shared session context. Shrink all context toward zero at the start of a session. Unlike the spectral kernel in §454, the decoder stores a fixed-size summary and learns its signal representation and readout jointly. The waveform studies §439/441 tested gain augmentation with a different memory, and §449 centered A6 writes after fitting. F2 tests subtracting the static head's expected class embedding from the shared context write during end-to-end learning. That change tests whether earlier trials indicate session style after removing predicted task composition. The prototype writes remain identical in form. Both arms train the same auxiliary class templates and have the same optimizer, episodes and seeds.

- Fit the other nine people in each public fold, with balanced interface/class source loss, 600 shuffled single-session episodes of 96 unique windows, two seeds (460 and 1460), AdamW 0.001 with weight decay 0.001. F1 is `prototype`, F2 is `centered`. Freeze the trained model before opening any held labels. Replay ten held people and all eight pinned §430 deliveries on Galvani. P04/P10 here are held-new roles.
- Compare whole waveform scores for genuine history, an equal-age cyclic source-session donor (different person, fixed canonical source session) and blank history. Also test each decoder as one terminal member of the complete historical three-eye/three-rest cap250 (§430): add 0.20 times the decoder's centered raw logits clipped to ±3 to the authenticated terminal log probabilities. Preserve all upstream stages and routes. Include exact-zero stock and matched decoder donor and blank arms. No blend weight selection.
- F1 predictions in percentage points: whole genuine minus donor +1.2 (80% −0.7 to +3.1), whole genuine minus blank +0.8 (−0.8 to +2.4), full-stack genuine minus stock +0.20 (−0.45 to +0.85), full-stack genuine minus blank +0.10 (−0.3 to +0.5). F2 predictions: F2 minus F1 full-stack genuine +0.12 (−0.35 to +0.59), improvement over matched blank specifically +0.10 (−0.35 to +0.55), F2 full-stack genuine minus stock +0.30 (−0.45 to +1.05). Positive paired people/order 80% lower bounds at both seeds support a directional claim, nonpositive uppers at both refute it, otherwise undecided. A whole replacement needs at least stock point performance before external qualification. Any additive result here must pass an independent cohort and actual staged ZIP evaluation before release.
