## Coverage and gaps

| Axis | What the record supports | Where coverage remains thin |

| Representation and supervision | We tried raw temporal models, compact Fourier models, spectral initialization, masked prediction, synthetic covariance variation, motor transfer, learned session memory, and distillation. Stronger raw FBCNet provided a much higher starting point than the initial prototypes. | Most early experiments started from weaker decoders. Few methods jointly changed the strong representation, training objective and adaptation. The specific masked and synthetic pilots remain weak or mixed evidence for their broader families. |
| Integration | We tested many final output blends and fixed selectors. We also tested bounded history in the six actual cap250 raw members, with an exact external change of −0.255 pp. | F-C13 tested a native replacement at 1/90 of the base mix. | Data and generalization | PROTEUS supplies ten fit-excluded public people. Scherer supplies nine external people excluded from the waveform fits. Reordered streams test robustness to delivery. | Both cohorts inform repeated choices. More seeds and permutations improve repeatability estimates on those people. A predeclared new, task-compatible cohort would test generalization beyond the search data. |
| Error recovery | A label-aware oracle found +2.285 pp available across four fixed alternatives on Scherer. Actual causal selectors recovered little or failed to replicate. The F5 direct error corrector lost 3.28–3.98 pp against exact stock. | The remaining question is whether different EEG evidence can recover errors without damaging correct predictions. A better standalone score and an oracle ceiling do not quantify that trade. |
| Runtime and more complex learning | Exact worker tests measured deployment overhead and several causal adaptation policies. Local retrieval can be cheap. The original spectral retrieval's stronger public result did not replicate in the exact Scherer package. | More compute helps when it enables a discriminating experiment or a measured better model. RL would need a meaningful causal action and a fixed-cost rollout control. Text-conditioned EEG generation would need real paired data and real held transfer. Neither belongs on the critical path for this Friday decision. |

## Next decisions

1. **Use the corrected complete-system baseline before selecting a Friday variant.** Check whether the replay correction changes the measured advantage of cap250 over v5.1b. Extend the paired evidence across orders, keep returning-person results separate, and compare any candidate in the complete worker at measured runtime. An isolated one-order result sets the next diagnostic and leaves rank uncertain.
2. **Test a different input adaptation in the actual worker.** Limit the influence of extreme covariance directions before they enter the running alignment sum. Compare with stock and a scalar control that removes the same total variance. Check whether the change discards useful EEG structure and measure complete RTX proxy time. The A100 pilot passed, with three cap events and one terminal label change in both variants. It used one 120-window session and no labels. [F-C12](cap250c12/result-18nov.md)
3. **Measure raw-only representation changes on held people.** The native fixed-weight swap recovered 24 stock errors and broke 22 correct stock decisions in its primary arm. Preserve stock as Friday's reference. Establish a whole-model held-person gain before testing a new native replacement. The prior binary Scherer specialist failed as a terminal correction. A class-aware head trained directly for its weighted ensemble contribution remains a separate hypothesis.

## Thursday evidence update · 19 November, 10:22 CET

The full-system hard replacement is the most reproducible exploratory improvement on the held-new instrument. It gained +0.425 and +0.374 pp versus exact cap250 in matched independent A100 source folds. The unchanged H100 hard archive improved +0.050 pp on independent exact external executions with both stock repeats, and its paired interval spans zero. Its independent numeric qualification remains open. [Held-new](fbc323_modulation_a100/result-19nov.md) · [Exact external](cap250_hard_exact/README.md)

Several different explanations for a stronger standalone waveform now have controls. Learned signed sensor covariance and carrier-independent envelopes failed to consistently beat exact hard. Soft source-person balance improved standalone decoding while the equal-dose mirror also improved. In corrected cap250, balance started −0.042 pp behind hard after routing in within0. CountFilter and CSP jointly deepened this gap by −0.229 pp. Full0 showed a small positive initial difference and almost no net change through both stages. A source intervention can improve a standalone decoder and alter the errors that challenge the live stack. The whole native trajectory is the unit of selection. [Sensor covariance](fbc323_covariance/result-19nov.md) · [Envelope](fbc323_envelope/result-19nov.md) · [Balance by stage](cap250_f35/result-19nov.md#where-the-standalone-effect-changed)

Three open tests address different gaps. F36 isolates trial-to-trial variation at matched raw waveform displacement. F-C31 asks whether a physical local sensor reconstruction exposes recoverable hard/stock disagreements relative to a lagged control. The native source-capture diagnostic targets the actual five-slot weighted mixer, which the earlier terminal corrector and standalone loss never optimized. The available archives do not support a nested fit-excluded conditional objective today. A new exact source tape could support all-public fitting with Scherer held out, if its real native reconstruction passes. Analysis remains on the pinned cohorts. [Friedrich acquisition](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22289414/)

The bounded standalone motor, balance and same-trial waveform changes all had positive point estimates at both seeds. Their complete cap250 variants lost in five of six pairings against matched hard. The same-trial crossfade lost on both corrected instruments with paired intervals below zero. This narrows the reason to keep testing interventions with the actual cap250 mixture and consuming stages. The conditional source objective is the direct counterpart to that finding.
